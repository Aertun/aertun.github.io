OpenID Connect

aertun oidc configure --issuer https://login.example.com --client-id aertun \
    --redirect-uri https://aertun.example.com/auth/callback [--mfa-acr ...]
aertun oidc configure --provider google|microsoft [--tenant ID] --client-id aertun \
    --redirect-uri https://aertun.example.com/auth/callback
aertun oidc check
aertun oidc status

The provider's issuer is discovered, its keys fetched, and the sign-in uses the authorization code flow with PKCE. --claim chooses whether the person is their verified email or their subject; an unverified email is refused unless you say otherwise.

A hosted provider is reached over the public internet and nothing else. A provider run inside the organisation — Keycloak, AD FS — is in a private range, which Aertun refuses to reach by default; --reach 10.20.0.0/16 names the ranges it may reach for discovery, keys and the token call, and no others. No range reaches link-local, where cloud metadata lives.

Two factors

A sign-in through the identity provider counts as two factors only when the provider says it used them: for OpenID Connect, the amr and acr claims with a recent auth_time; for SAML, the authentication context. When it does not say, Aertun asks for the person's passkey as well.
